# The World — erg, Frontier-64, three powers

A commander's brief. The full canon lives in [LORE.md](../../LORE.en.md);
contradicting it is a bug. In game, the world is told by the Lore
archive in the main menu (entries unlock as the war unfolds).

![The Lore archive](img/todo-codex.md)

## Erg

In the year 2189 humanity runs on erg — the energy of crystal fields.
An erg crystal grows in only three places of the Sabgir sector.
Whoever owns the fields feeds half the system. In the interface humans
call erg "minerals", the Kharid call it "facets": to the hive, crystals
are not ore but sleeping kin.

## Frontier-64

The sixty-fourth outpost on the edge of settled space: a line of bases,
quarries and silent watchtowers from Aktan-4 to its gray moon. Beyond
them — only the swarm. Motto of the watchtowers: "The Frontier holds;
the erg flows."

## Three powers

- **Helios Industries** — a mining corporation with an army of its own:
  an expeditionary corps, hover vehicles, plasma. Sector command — the
  port of Helios Prime. Engineers and miners who believed the Kharid
  were fauna — until the dunes rose. In swarm intercepts: "the iron kind".
- **The Kharid** — natives of Brok-Thaal, a half-crystalline swarm:
  every creature is one facet of a single brood, bound by the brood-song.
  Their war is sacred: they take their children back. The Dusk nest was
  first to sell facets for machines and fuel — and joined the mutiny.
- **The Red Flag** — a third of the Artemis garrison, risen in mutiny.
  They call themselves Sons of the Sand. Their commanders are former
  Helios officers. Their stake is the Silencer: a device that dampens
  the erg fields of the region. Capital — the warehouse city of Flint.

## Three planets

- **Aktan-4** — Helios's red planet: the Rudnik-7 quarry, the Zarya-9
  camp, the Ash Pass, Artemis, Nail Fort, Helios Prime. The war began
  here — with the Delta-2 mine falling silent.
- **Brok-Thaal** — the hive's violet world: sacred fields, the Lower
  and Upper Fields, the Dusk nest. Foreign boots here are blasphemy.
- **Selenite-B** — a gray moon: the Lazur crater, Outpost Rampart, the
  anchor of the orbital elevator, and the Eye of the Storm rift — the
  mutiny's last den.
