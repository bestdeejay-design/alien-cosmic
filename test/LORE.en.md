# The Alien Cosmic Legend: Frontier-64

> *Everything seen in the game — concept, story, legend. This document
> is the single canon (the Russian LORE.md keeps priority; this EN
> mirror follows it line for line). The texts in the code — names,
> descriptions, briefings, toasts — must match it; a contradiction
> to the canon is a bug.*

## 0. Prologue: erg

In the year 2189 humanity runs on erg — the energy of crystal fields.
An erg crystal grows in only three places of the Sabgir sector, and
all three are alien worlds. Whoever owns the fields feeds half the
system. Whoever loses them — goes dark.

Frontier-64 — the sixty-fourth outpost on the edge of settled space:
a line of bases, quarries and silent watchtowers from Aktan-4 to its
gray moon. Beyond them — only the swarm.

## 1. Helios Industries

Full name — Helios Industries, shorter — Helios, in the swarm's
intercepts — "the iron kind". A mining corporation with an army of
its own: an expeditionary corps, hover vehicles, plasma, orbital
ports. Sector command — the port of Helios Prime.

Helios are not poster villains. They are engineers, miners and
officers who honestly believed the Kharid were mindless fauna — right
up until the dunes rose and marched on the camps. Their tragedy is
blindness. Their valor is the line: they do not abandon their own,
even when the shuttles are already late.

- Motto of the watchtowers: *"The Frontier holds; the erg flows."*
- The machines' colors — steel and the turquoise of signal lights;
  amber is the color of price, construction and hazard stripes.

## 2. The Kharid, the crystalborn

Natives of Brok-Thaal. A half-crystalline swarm-organism: every
creature is one facet of a single brood, bound by the **brood-song** —
a bioresonance network that Helios translators mistook for static for
a long time. To the hive, minerals are not ore but **sleeping kin**:
unhatched facets. Every crystal lifted from a field is a stolen child.

That is why the war is sacred: the Kharid are not defending territory,
they are taking their children back. Nests are not barracks but
maternity wards; the Mandibles are not guns but the maws of the
watch facets; the Evolution Node is where the swarm decides what it
will become tomorrow.

- The split: the Dusk nest was first to break the silence pact and
  began trading facets with the mutineers. The splinter brood went
  over to the Red Flag — all the way to the Last Song fortress.
- The swarm's colors — the violet of flesh and the green of slime;
  the hive's heart beats red.

## 3. The Red Flag (Sons of the Sand)

A third of the Artemis station garrison, risen in mutiny at the height
of the war. They call themselves **Sons of the Sand** — children of
Aktan tired of dying for dividends. Everyone else calls them **the Red
Flag** — after the banners on the captured watchtowers. Their
commanders are former Helios officers: they know the drill books, the
base schematics and the weak points of every turret.

They trade with both sides, but their aim is higher: **the Silencer**
— a device that dampens the erg fields of a whole region. Without erg
both the corporation and the swarm fall — and then the Sons of the
Sand remain the only power of the sector. Capital — the warehouse city
of Flint; last fort — the Eye of the Storm rift on Selenite.

## 4. The planets

**Aktan-4** — Helios's red planet: rusted deserts, salt seas (the
Sabgir sea), the Rudnik-7 quarry, the Zarya-9 camp, the Ash Pass, the
Artemis station, Nail Fort, the port of Helios Prime. The war began
here — with the Delta-2 mine falling silent.

**Brok-Thaal** — the hive's violet world: the sacred crystal fields,
the Lower Fields (where the brood first saw light), the Upper Fields
(the heart of the sacred hunt), the fields of Sabgir, the Dusk nest.
Foreign boots here are blasphemy.

**Selenite-B** — a gray moon over Aktan: the Lazur crater, Outpost
Rampart by the rift entrance, the anchor of the orbital elevator — and
the Eye of the Storm rift, the Red Flag's last den.

## 5. Heroes and places (for briefings and the codex)

- **Delta-2 Mine** — it stopped answering; the first words of the
  survivors were "the dunes are alive".
- **Zarya-9 Camp** — the night of spikes; the line at the Ash Gorge.
- **The Ash Pass** — the only road to the sea; the evacuation of the region.
- **Artemis** — a transfer warehouse; the birthplace of the mutiny.
- **Nail Fort** — entrenched defectors; the Novas' first engagement.
- **The Dusk Nest** — the swarm that sold itself for machines and fuel.
- **Outpost Rampart** — an orbital elevator raised under fire.
- **Flint** — capital of the Sons of the Sand; the order read "total rout".
- **Helios Prime** — home. To defend the port is to defend everything.
- **Eye of the Storm** — the mutiny's last fort; the first joint strike
  of humans and swarm.
- **The Last Song** — a fortress-nest on the fields of Sabgir where the
  Silencer is hidden. The war's finale — and the beginning of peace.

## 6. The arsenal: who is who

**Helios.** The Tech Drone — a repair hover, the base's hands. The
Ranger — assault trooper, the backbone of the line. The Skiff — raider
of the flanks. The Bastion — a heavy fist. The Nanite — a swarm of
medics. The Plasmer — a flamer crew. The Nova Mortar — siege thunder.
The Pathfinder — the base's eyes: a light hover with a spotter drone.
The Viper Turret — an automatic sentinel: fires in a pair, like a
viper's two fangs (the callsign came from the Zarya-9 gun crews).

**The Kharid.** The Gatherer — a crystal-bearer, nurse of the fields.
The Shardjaw — a creeping marksman. The Skitter — a predator of raids.
The Bruiser — a colossus with horns. The Bloom — a nursing spore turned
field medic. The Acid Spitter — a living flamethrower. The Sporehulk —
a worm-mortar. The Listener — the swarm's ears: a nimble little beast
with a companion spore. The Mandibles — a watchful maw. The Cocoon —
a living shelter: fighters inside are invulnerable and sting through
the seams.

## 7. Campaign timeline

- **Act I "Frontier"** — invasion and answer: Delta-2, the night of
  spikes, the swarm's awakening, the quarry, the evacuation through
  the Ash Pass.
- **Act II "Fire and Steel"** — the third power: the sacred fields,
  the Artemis mutiny, Nail Fort, the raid on Dusk, the rise of Rampart.
- **Act III "Dominion"** — the price of peace: the heart of the hive,
  the rout of Flint, the defense of Helios Prime, the Eye of the Storm,
  the Last Song.
- Finale: the Silencer destroyed, the Frontier closed. The corporation
  and the swarm look at each other without a gun-sight for the first time.

## 8. Glossary (canonical spellings)

- **erg** (lowercase) — the energy; **erg crystal**, **erg field**.
- **facet** — a crystal in the Kharid's speech; **the sleeping** —
  unhatched facets.
- **the brood-song** — the swarm's bond. **The brood** — the swarm as a whole.
- **Frontier-64 / the Frontier** — the line of outposts; lowercase
  "frontier" — a defensive line.
- **Helios Industries / Helios / "the iron kind"** (in the swarm's speech).
- **the Kharid / the swarm / the hive**; the Dusk nest — always with "the".
- **the Red Flag / Sons of the Sand** (their self-name).
- **the Silencer**, **Nova**, **Viper** — canonical names as approved.
- Minerals in the interface — the game name of erg for humans; for the
  Kharid — "facets". The strings live in the `messages` section of
  `locales/ru.js` / `locales/en.js` (package I moved them out of code).
