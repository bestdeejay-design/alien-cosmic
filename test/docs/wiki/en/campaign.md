# The "Stellar Frontier" campaign — 15 missions of three acts

The classic arc: every new unit is introduced by a mission built around
it. Below — chapter, side, place, objective and the intro; tactics and
write-ups live in `GAME_GUIDE.en.md`, without walkthrough spoilers.

![Mission select](img/todo-missions.md)

## Act I "Frontier" — the landing and the first wounds

| № | Mission | Side | Place | Objective |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A Strange Shore | Helios | Aktan-4, the Delta region | Tear down a Kharid nest |
| 2 | Night of Spikes | Helios | The Zarya-9 camp | Survive 10:00, then load 6 soldiers |
| 3 | The Depths Awaken | Kharid | Brok-Thaal, the sacred fields | Crush the Camp Heart (CC) |
| 4 | Rudnik-7 | Helios | Aktan-4, the Rudnik-7 plateau | Stockpile 2,500 erg |
| 5 | The Ash Line | Helios | The Ash Pass | Carry 8 soldiers out (3s in the ring each) |

Act debuts: rangers (№1), turrets/walls/bunkers (№2), the factory with
skiffs and bastions (№3). In №3 the punitive corps arrives by the
seventh minute — hurry.

## Objective types (cheat sheet)

- **Demolition** — destroy every enemy structure (or the diamond-marked
  key target: an HQ, storehouses, a node).
- **Defense** — survive to the timer; razing the hive early wins too.
- **Stockpile** — hold the erg figure in your treasury.
- **Evacuation** — keep soldiers inside the ring of lights for the
  required seconds.
- **Sabotage** — a small squad without a base: losses never come back.
