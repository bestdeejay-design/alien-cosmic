# The "Stellar Frontier" wiki — a commander's reference (version I)

Everything Season 1 has: units, buildings, upgrades, mechanics, the
campaign, controls and the world. Numbers come from the code
(`js/config.js`, `js/missions.js`) and were verified line by line;
nothing is invented. Text-vs-code discrepancies live in
[CONSISTENCY.md](../CONSISTENCY.md) (internal, Russian-only). Version I
adds the English localization and this EN mirror of the wiki.

![The main menu with seasons](img/todo-main-menu.md)

## How to read: two looks

Every role in the game has one stat line and two looks. The humans of
Helios Industries speak in drill-book and engineering terms, the Kharid
in the brood's song. In this wiki the Helios look comes first, the
swarm's second:

**Tech Drone / Gatherer** — the human on the left, the Kharid on the right.

Proper names follow the canon of [LORE.en.md](../../LORE.en.md):
erg is lowercase, the Silencer, Nova Mortar, Viper Turret carry their
canonical forms, Sons of the Sand without quotes.
